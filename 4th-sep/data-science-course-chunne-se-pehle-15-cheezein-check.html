SECTION 015 High Priority checks – Must-have items

Yeh 5 cheezein course me honi chahiye — warna course worth nahi hai:

1. Curriculum relevance High

Must HaveCurriculum

Curriculum 2026 ke industry standards ke hisaab se updated hona chahiye. ML, DL, NLP, CV, cloud — sab cover ho.

Check: Latest tools, frameworks, and real-world case studies in the curriculum.

2. Tools & frameworks High

Must HaveTools

Python, SQL, TensorFlow/PyTorch, scikit-learn, pandas, numpy, cloud platforms — sab cover hone chahiye.

Must have: Python, SQL, TensorFlow, PyTorch, scikit-learn, AWS/GCP/Azure.

3. Projects & portfolio High

Must HaveProjects

8+ projects, 3-4 portfolio-ready, real datasets, end-to-end implementation — projects are what get you hired.

Check: Project list, datasets used, portfolio guidelines, mentor feedback.

4. Faculty & mentors High

Must HaveFaculty

Industry-experienced faculty, 1:1 mentorship, doubt sessions, office hours — learning quality depends on faculty.

Check: Faculty profiles, industry experience, student reviews about mentors.

5. Placement support High

Must HavePlacement

Resume building, mock interviews, hiring partners, job referrals, placement cell — job-ready support essential.

Check: Placement record, hiring partners, resume support, mock interview process.
Key insight: Yeh 5 cheezein MUST-HAVE hain. Agar course me inme se koi missing hai, toh aage mat badhein.

SECTION 025 Medium Priority checks – Important items

Yeh 5 cheezein important hain but negotiable:

6. Certification value Medium

ImportantCertification

Course certification industry-recognized hona chahiye. Not all certificates are equal — check the issuing authority.

Check: Who issues the certificate? Is it recognized by employers?

7. Class size & batch Medium

ImportantBatch

Small batch size (20-30 students) is better for personalized attention. Large batches mean less interaction.

Check: Batch size, student-teacher ratio, interaction opportunities.

8. Student reviews & ratings Medium

ImportantReviews

Independent reviews (Google, Trustpilot, Reddit) check karein. Be careful of fake reviews — look for detailed honest feedback.

Check: Multiple review platforms, detailed reviews, alumni testimonials.

9. Course cost & ROI Medium

ImportantROI

Course fee vs expected salary hike — ROI calculate karein. Compare fees with market rates for similar courses.

Check: Fee breakdown, payment options, expected salary after course.

10. Batch timing & schedule Medium

ImportantSchedule

Weekday vs weekend batches, timing flexibility, recording availability — choose according to your schedule.

Check: Batch timings, recording access, make-up classes.
Pro tip: Reviews and ROI are very important — don't skip these. Check multiple sources for reviews. Calculate ROI before committing.

SECTION 035 Nice-to-Have checks – Bonus features

Yeh 5 cheezein bonus hain — good to have but not essential:

11. Alumni network Nice

Nice to HaveAlumni

Strong alumni network helps with referrals, mentorship, and job opportunities. Check if they have active alumni groups.

12. Free resources & tools Nice

Nice to HaveResources

Free access to tools, datasets, cloud credits, libraries — additional resources that add value to the course.

13. Flexible schedule options Nice

Nice to HaveFlexibility

Self-paced options, weekend-only, evening batches, recording access — flexibility helps working professionals.

14. Industry connections Nice

Nice to HaveIndustry

Guest lectures, industry talks, hackathons, networking events — exposure to industry professionals and trends.

15. Job guarantee (if real) Nice

Nice to HaveGuarantee

Some courses offer job guarantees (with conditions). Be very careful — read terms carefully. Usually not worth it.

Key point: Nice-to-have features are bonus but should not be the deciding factor. Focus on high and medium priority checks first.

SECTION 04Course scoring framework – Kaise score karein

Multiple courses compare karne ke liye yeh scoring framework use karein:

High Priority (5 items) — weight 50%
Must have itemsScore: 4/5
Medium Priority (5 items) — weight 30%
Important itemsScore: 3/5
Nice-to-Have (5 items) — weight 20%
Bonus itemsScore: 2/5
  • Scoring: Each item = 1 point. Weighted score = (High score × 0.5) + (Medium score × 0.3) + (Nice score × 0.2) × 100%
  • 85%+: Excellent course, 70-84%: Good course, Below 70%: Look for better options.

SECTION 05Red flags to avoid – Kya nahi hona chahiye

Course select karte waqt yeh red flags avoid karein:

  • Outdated curriculum: No AI tools, no Python, no cloud — 2026 me outdated course.
  • No hands-on projects: Sirf theory — avoid karein. Practical projects zaroori hain.
  • Fake placement claims: "100% placement guarantee" — usually fake. Check actual placement data.
  • No mentor feedback: Projects ka review nahi hota — learning incomplete.
  • Too good to be true: Unrealistically low fees with "guaranteed" placement — be cautious.
  • No transparent information: Faculty profiles, curriculum, placement data hidden — avoid.
