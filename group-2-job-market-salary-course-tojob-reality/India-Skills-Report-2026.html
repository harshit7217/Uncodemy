SECTION 04Future Outlook — 2027 & Beyond

India skills landscape ka future outlook — key trends:

  • AI & Automation — 68% companies AI use kar rahi hain, demand for AI skills will grow.
  • Hybrid Work — 42% workforce hybrid model mein hai, permanent trend hai.
  • Skills over Degrees — 58% companies skills-based hiring kar rahi hain.
  • Green Economy — Sustainability jobs 15% growth ke saath fast growing.
Pro tip: Future mein AI skills, hybrid work adaptability, aur green skills sabse important hongi.
