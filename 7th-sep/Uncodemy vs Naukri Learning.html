SECTION 01Uncodemy AWS Course Overview

Uncodemy offers a comprehensive AWS course designed for beginners and professionals looking to build a career in cloud computing. Here's what you get:

  • Comprehensive curriculum: Covers EC2, S3, VPC, IAM, RDS, Lambda, CloudFormation, and more.
  • Hands-on projects: Build real-world AWS projects — 8+ live projects.
  • Placement support: 90%+ placement rate with top recruiters like Accenture, Amazon, Deloitte, and Flipkart.
  • Expert trainers: Learn from industry experts with 10+ years of cloud experience.
  • Affordable fees: ₹15,500 for the full programme — best value in Delhi NCR.
  • Flexible batches: Weekday and weekend batches available.
Key insight: Uncodemy offers the best value-for-money AWS course in Delhi NCR with excellent placement support.

SECTION 02Naukri Learning AWS Course Overview

Naukri Learning offers AWS certification courses through various partners. Here's what you can expect:

  • Partner-based delivery: Courses are delivered through third-party training partners.
  • Variable curriculum: Curriculum quality depends on the training partner.
  • Higher fees: Typically ₹25,000+ for AWS courses.
  • Limited placement: No dedicated placement support — you're on your own for job hunting.
  • Self-paced learning: Mostly self-paced with limited live sessions.
  • Certificate: Course completion certificate provided.
Pro tip: Naukri Learning is a good option if you prefer self-paced learning and don't need dedicated placement support. However, Uncodemy offers better value with live training and placement.

SECTION 06Interview Q&A — AWS Course Comparison

Q1Which is better — Uncodemy or Naukri Learning for AWS?

Uncodemy is better for most learners — it offers live training, comprehensive curriculum, lower fees, and excellent placement support. Naukri Learning is better only if you prefer self-paced learning and don't need job support.

Q2Why is Uncodemy cheaper than Naukri Learning?

Uncodemy offers direct training without middlemen, keeping costs low. Naukri Learning partners with third-party providers, which adds to the cost.

Q3Does Naukri Learning offer AWS placement?

Naukri Learning does not offer dedicated placement support. You get a certificate but need to find jobs on your own.

Q4Does Uncodemy offer AWS certification?

Yes — Uncodemy provides a course completion certificate and helps you prepare for AWS certifications like AWS Certified Solutions Architect, Developer, and SysOps Administrator.

Q5Which AWS course has better value for money?

Uncodemy offers the best value for money — lower fees, live training, projects, and placement support. You get more for your investment.
