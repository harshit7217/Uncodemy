SECTION 01Average Salary for Freshers in 2026

The average salary for an ethical hacking fresher in India ranges from ₹4.5 to ₹6 LPA. With certifications and skills, you can earn up to ₹8 LPA.

Role Average Salary (LPA) With CEH Certification With OSCP
Junior Ethical Hacker ₹4.5 - 5.5 ₹5.5 - 6.5 ₹6.5 - 8
Security Analyst ₹5 - 6 ₹6 - 7 ₹7 - 8.5
Penetration Tester (Junior) ₹5.5 - 6.5 ₹6.5 - 7.5 ₹7.5 - 9 
Ethical Hacking Salary Breakdown (Freshers):
- Base Salary: ₹4.5 - 6 LPA
- With CEH: ₹5.5 - 7 LPA
- With OSCP: ₹6.5 - 9 LPA
- With experience (0-2 years): ₹6 - 10 LPA

Bonuses & Benefits:
- Performance bonus: ₹50k - 1 Lakh
- Health insurance
- Training & certification sponsorship
- Flexible work hours
Skills that Boost Your Salary:
✅ Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)
✅ Offensive Security Certified Professional (OSCP)
✅ Python / Bash scripting
✅ Network security (Cisco, Firewalls)
✅ Cloud security (AWS, Azure)
✅ Vulnerability assessment tools (Nessus, Burp Suite)
✅ Penetration testing frameworks (Metasploit)

Key insight: Certifications like CEH and OSCP can increase your salary by 30-50%. Invest in them early.

SECTION 05Interview Q&A — Ethical Hacking

Q1What is the average salary for ethical hacking freshers in 2026?

The average salary for ethical hacking freshers in India is ₹4.5 - 6 LPA. With certifications like CEH or OSCP, you can earn up to ₹8 LPA.

Q2Which certification is best for a higher salary?

CEH (Certified Ethical Hacker) and OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional) are the most valued. OSCP generally leads to higher pay than CEH.

Q3How can I increase my ethical hacking salary?

Get certified (CEH, OSCP), learn in-demand skills (cloud security, Python), build a strong portfolio, and consider working with startups or top MNCs.

Q4Which city pays the highest for ethical hacking?

Bangalore and Mumbai offer the highest salaries for ethical hacking freshers, ranging from ₹5.5 to ₹7 LPA.

Q5Can I earn more as a freelancer?

Yes. Freelance ethical hacking can pay ₹10k-50k per project, depending on complexity. However, it requires experience and a strong reputation.

SECTION 07Frequently asked questions

What's the starting salary for ethical hacking freshers in 2026?

The starting salary is ₹4.5 - 6 LPA on average. With certifications, it can go up to ₹8 LPA.

Do I need a degree to become an ethical hacker?

Not necessarily. Many ethical hackers are self-taught. However, a degree in computer science or IT can help you get past HR filters and command a higher salary.

What's the salary growth for ethical hacking?

With 2-3 years of experience, you can earn ₹8-12 LPA. Senior roles can pay ₹15-20 LPA or more.

Which companies pay the most to freshers?

IBM, Accenture, and top cybersecurity startups pay the highest, ranging from ₹6 to ₹8 LPA for freshers.

Is remote work available for ethical hacking freshers?

Yes, many companies offer remote or hybrid work. Remote salaries may be slightly lower than metro salaries but can be more cost-effective.
