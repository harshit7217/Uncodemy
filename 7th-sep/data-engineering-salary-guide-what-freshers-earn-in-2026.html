SECTION 05Interview Q&A — Data Engineer Salary

Q1What is the average salary for a fresher data engineer in India?

The average salary for a fresher data engineer (0-2 years) is ₹7-14 LPA, with the average being around ₹10 LPA.

Q2Which city pays the most for data engineers?

Bengaluru offers the highest salaries for data engineers, followed by Mumbai and Delhi-NCR. Bengaluru also has the most opportunities.

Q3What skills increase salary the most?

Cloud platforms (AWS/GCP/Azure), Spark, Kafka, and Airflow are the skills that increase salary the most. Python and SQL are also essential.

Q4Can I earn more as a data engineer or data scientist?

Data scientists and ML engineers typically earn slightly more than data engineers at senior levels. However, data engineering has less competition and more stable demand.

Q5What is the salary growth potential for data engineers?

Data engineers can progress from ₹7-14 LPA as freshers to ₹50-75 LPA as senior professionals, and up to ₹100+ LPA in executive roles.

SECTION 07Frequently asked questions

What is the starting salary for a data engineer in India?

The starting salary for a data engineer in India is typically ₹6-10 LPA, with top companies offering up to ₹14 LPA for freshers.

Which city has the highest data engineering salaries?

Bengaluru has the highest data engineering salaries, followed by Mumbai and Delhi-NCR. Remote work is also becoming more common.

What skills increase data engineering salary?

Cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure), Spark, Kafka, Airflow, and Python are the top skills that increase data engineering salaries.

How much can a senior data engineer earn?

A senior data engineer (5-8 years) can earn ₹35-55 LPA, with top performers earning ₹50-75 LPA at the principal level.

Is data engineering a good career for freshers?

Yes, data engineering is one of the best careers for freshers. It offers high salaries, strong demand, and clear career progression paths.
