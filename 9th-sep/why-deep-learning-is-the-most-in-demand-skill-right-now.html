SECTION 06Frequently asked questions

Why is deep learning in such high demand?

Deep learning powers generative AI, computer vision, NLP, and more — technologies that are transforming every industry. Companies are investing heavily in AI, creating massive demand for talent.

What is the salary range for deep learning roles?

Entry-level roles start at ₹12-18 LPA, while senior positions can go up to ₹50-80 LPA+ in India.

Do I need a degree to become a deep learning engineer?

Not necessarily. While a degree in CS, Math, or Data Science helps, many companies prioritize skills and portfolio over formal degrees.

How long does it take to learn deep learning?

With consistent effort, you can become job-ready in 6-9 months. The timeline depends on your prior experience with programming and math.

Which framework should I learn — PyTorch or TensorFlow?

Both are popular. PyTorch is the favorite in research and academia, while TensorFlow is widely used in production. Learn both if possible.
