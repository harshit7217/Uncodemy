SECTION 06Interview Q&A — AWS Careers

Q1Is Uncodemy or Coursera better for AWS?

It depends on your needs. Uncodemy is better for students and beginners looking for live classes, hands-on training, and placement support. Coursera is better for self-learners who want flexibility and university certificates.

Q2What is the average AWS cloud engineer salary in India?

Entry-level AWS engineers earn ₹5-10 LPA, mid-level earn ₹10-18 LPA, and senior AWS architects earn ₹18-35 LPA+.

Q3Does Uncodemy offer placement support for AWS?

Yes, Uncodemy has 14+ hiring partners and offers comprehensive placement support including resume building, mock interviews, and job referrals.

Q4What AWS certifications are most valuable?

AWS Certified Solutions Architect, AWS Developer, and AWS SysOps Administrator are the most valuable certifications. Uncodemy helps prepare for these certifications.

Q5How long does it take to become an AWS engineer?

With Uncodemy's 3-4 month course and placement support, most students get their first AWS job within 4-6 months. With Coursera's self-paced learning, it may take longer but offers more flexibility.
