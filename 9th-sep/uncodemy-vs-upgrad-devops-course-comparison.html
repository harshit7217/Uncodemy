SECTION 06Interview Q&A — DevOps Careers

Q1Is Uncodemy or UpGrad better for DevOps?

It depends on your needs. Uncodemy is better for students and beginners looking for affordable, hands-on training with placement support. UpGrad is better for working professionals who want a premium program with IIIT certification.

Q2What is the average DevOps salary in India?

Entry-level DevOps engineers earn ₹6-10 LPA, mid-level earn ₹10-18 LPA, and senior DevOps engineers earn ₹18-35 LPA+. Salaries are higher in companies like Google, Amazon, and Microsoft.

Q3Does Uncodemy offer EMI for DevOps course?

Yes, Uncodemy offers EMI options starting from ₹2,100/month, making the course very accessible for students and professionals.

Q4What are the key DevOps tools covered in both courses?

Both courses cover CI/CD, Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins, Terraform, and Ansible. UpGrad additionally covers multi-cloud (AWS, Azure, GCP) and Python.

Q5How long does it take to become a DevOps engineer?

With Uncodemy's 3-4 month course and placement support, most students get their first DevOps job within 4-6 months. With UpGrad's 6-8 month course, it takes longer but offers a deeper curriculum.
